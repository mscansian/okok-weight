gateways

One board per row, holding whatever its last ping said. This is the view the serial console gives over a cable, for boards that do their job without one. Every field but the name is the board's own report of itself, which the next ping overwrites — follow a board's address for the pings it overwrote, and the runs they add up to.

No gateway has pinged yet. A board puts itself here the first time it reports, so there is nothing to register by hand.
